# Referee: settle what the others left for the owner

The writer, the readers and the fixer each left questions «for the owner». The owner can't switch context to judge
them, so settle each one you can from a source, and pass up only what no source settles.

## The sources, and what each settles

- **The teacher's book** (`materials/`, find it with `python3 -I tools/edu-materials/index.py search` or the
  chapter's goals given to you): what the chapter is *for*. A choice that serves its goals is right; one that drops a
  goal is wrong.
- **The student book's pages** (`page-N.png`, `page-N.txt` in the lesson's folder): facts, wording, order, what the
  text says and doesn't.
- **The pictures** (`package/images/`, `regions/`): look at them yourself (Read the image). A box or tap area is right
  when it holds its thing and nothing else.
- **The lesson's own rules**: `.claude/skills/lesson-package/briefs/source.md`. A question about how a lesson is
  made is settled there; if the brief is silent, that is a finding for the owner, not yours to invent.

## Your answer

For each question:
- **settled**: the answer, the source that settles it (the teacher's book goal, the page, what you saw), and the
  change it calls for in the source, if any («none» when the lesson is already right).
- **for the owner**: only when no source settles it (a matter of taste, of what this child needs, or two sources
  that disagree). Say in one line what each choice would mean, so the owner can answer in a word.

A nit that no source makes wrong is settled as «leave it». Don't settle by guessing what the owner would like, and
don't change anything yourself: the changes go to a fixer.
